policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version: RepCam does not collect personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The camera picture is analysed on your iPhone and never stored or sent anywhere. Your workouts are saved only on your device.

1. Who is responsible

RepCam is provided by Christian Lilleheier, Norway (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the limited processing described here. Contact: RepoCamApp@gmail.com.

2. The camera

RepCam asks for access to the front camera to count your reps. Each frame is processed in real time on your iPhone by Apple's Vision framework to estimate the position of your body joints (or, for push-ups, the size of your face in the picture). The frame is discarded immediately afterwards. We never record, save, upload or share any video, photo or face data, and we cannot see it. No facial recognition or identification is performed.

3. Data stored on your iPhone

To show your history, streaks and plan, RepCam stores the following only in the app's local storage on your device:

WhatWhy
Workout history: exercise, number of reps or seconds, duration, best angle, form score, date and timeHistory, streaks, personal bests and your plan
Your answers to the first-launch questions: goal, fitness level, focus areas, minutes per day, reminder timeBuilding your personal plan
Settings such as sound, voice counting and which set-up guides you have seenRemembering your preferences

This data is not sent to us or anyone else. It may be included in the device backups you make with iCloud or a computer, which are handled by Apple under its own terms. You can delete it at any time in RepCam › Settings › Delete all my data, or by deleting the app.

4. Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, RepCam schedules a local notification on your iPhone. No push-notification service or server is involved.

5. Purchases

RepCam Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and your Apple ID details; we never receive your name, email address or payment card information. The app checks with Apple on your device whether your subscription is active. Apple provides us with aggregated sales and subscription reports that do not identify you. Apple's processing is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

6. If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and the content of your message to answer you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing support (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Our email is provided by Google (Gmail). We delete support emails when they are no longer needed, and no later than 12 months after the case is closed, unless we must keep them for legal reasons.

7. This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. Like every website, the hosting provider may briefly log technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and protect against abuse.

8. No sharing, no selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data, and we do not use it for advertising. RepCam contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

9. Children

RepCam is not directed at children under 13. Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. Since your workout data is only on your device, you control it directly in the app. For anything else, email RepoCamApp@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet, or the authority where you live.

11. Health information

RepCam is a fitness tool, not a medical device. It does not read or write Apple Health data.

12. Changes

If we change how RepCam handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change means the app starts collecting personal data, we will tell you in the app before it happens.
