Support

We usually reply within two working days.

Email RepoCamApp@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, the exercise and what happened — a screenshot helps.

Reps are not counted

  • Tap the ? button during a set to see where the phone should be for that exercise.
  • Your whole body (head to feet) should be in the picture, 2–3 m away for standing exercises and about 1.5 m for floor exercises.
  • For push-ups, lay the phone flat on the floor, screen up, under your face.
  • Good light helps. Avoid bright windows behind you.

Restore Pro on a new iPhone

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open RepCam › Settings › Restore purchases.

Cancel or change your subscription

Settings on your iPhone › your name › Subscriptions › RepCam. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your data

RepCam › Settings › Delete all my data removes your workouts, streaks and answers from the iPhone. We don't hold a copy. See the privacy policy.
